      AI Assessment

      See your AI profile before day one.

      The assessment is practical, not a trivia test. You work through realistic tasks and we map how you work across six core competencies, then use that profile to match your mission and mentor.

      AI Fluency
      Directs AI tools well and knows when to trust, check or reject the output.
      Learning Velocity
      Goes from zero to useful on a new tool or domain in days, not weeks.
      Ownership
      Treats outcomes as their own, follows through, and flags problems early.
      Ambiguity Comfort
      Makes progress when the brief is incomplete and the answer isn't known yet.
      Builder Instinct
      Defaults to making something, a prototype or a workflow, instead of debating it.
      Communication Clarity
      Explains work, trade-offs and results so others can act on them.
